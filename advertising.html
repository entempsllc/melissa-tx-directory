Featured Listings & Local Sponsorships

Melissa-area businesses and community organizations may ask about paid visibility on this independent local directory.

Options available for discussion

  • Featured listing: enhanced placement using accurate information supplied or confirmed by the business.
  • Local sponsorship: clearly identified support associated with an agreed directory section or placement.

Paid placements will be clearly labeled. Payment does not buy a positive review, rating, verification claim, or editorial endorsement.

Availability, placement, duration, deliverables, and any fee must be agreed in writing before publication. We do not guarantee impressions, clicks, leads, rankings, or sales.

Ask about a featured listing or sponsorship

Please include your business or organization name, website, and the type of placement you want to discuss. Sending an inquiry creates no obligation.
